How the six colours were applied

  • #B22222 replaces the violet everywhere it is the brand: primary buttons, links, the active tab, the selected segment, the focus ring, the landing and home-header gradients (with a lighter #C83333 and a deeper #6B1414 derived from it), and the soft tints (#F9EAEA, #FDF5F5).
  • #141414 is the heading colour and also the dark “ink” for the interview cards, so they become neutral black instead of navy. #2C2C2C is labels and body copy, #777777 the muted copy, #E5E5E5 every hairline and #FCFCFC the page.
  • Feed: the pink highlight (pay and job-type pills, Pass, the film progress and filter count) becomes the brand red. Green Save and yellow Undo stay as they are.
  • Green (published / live / completion) and amber (warnings) stay as they are.
  • Open point: today's error red #C22A30 is almost the same as the new primary. Errors and the late-join red card would look like normal buttons. See the urgent scorecard row on screen 4.
