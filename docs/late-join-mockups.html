Late join — warning, negative timer and red cards

Wherever a Join option is shown, once the scheduled start has passed and you haven't joined, the card says how late you are and the clock runs below zero (−02:14). After 5 minutes it turns red. The card also says whether the other side is in the room. Drawn for the student and the interviewer, on the app and the web, in Geist with normal-case labels. Switch the state in the bar; the red treatment has two options.

Before the startJoin open: “Starts in 04:12”, as today.
0–5 min late (amber)“You're 2 min late”, the clock at −02:14, and when Join closes.
Over 5 min late (red)The whole card turns red; the clock and the close time stay.
The other side“Interviewer is in the room” / “Interviewer not in yet · 3 min late”. Student sees no name — the interviewer stays unnamed until the session starts.
