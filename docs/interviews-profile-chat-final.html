My interviews

Filter chips with counts, a card per interview, the next one highlighted with its countdown, and a Book button that shrinks to an icon as you scroll. Tap a row to see the interview’s detail screen.

List screen
Detail screen (what each row opens)
Production notes

What makes it ship-ready

  • States: normal, loading skeleton, empty (“Nothing booked yet.”), error with Try again, and a filtered-empty line.
  • Every status: Booked, In progress, Completed, Under review, Cancelled, Rescheduled, No show.
  • Next interview carries a violet card and its countdown, derived from the real start time.
  • Scrolling: the large title collapses into a compact bar; the Book button shrinks to “+”.
  • Touch: chips 40px, rows ≥ 100px, buttons ≥ 44px; focus rings for keyboard and switch access.
  • Pull to refresh stays as it is today.
  • No shadows; depth comes from borders and tints.

Profile

The Profile tab, the Account screen: who you are, your destinations, identity with per-identifier verification, settings, and Sign out with confirmation.

Account screen
Production notes

What makes it ship-ready

  • Identity is shown per identifier, at the identifier: email and mobile each carry their own Verified state.
  • Unverified email shows the one violet Verify button, then a “Link sent” state. It explains why: you can’t pay or book another interview until it’s verified.
  • Sign out asks first (“Are you sure you want to sign out?”) and says it applies to this device only.
  • Delete or export my data is an ordinary row, not red; the destructive control lives on its own screen.
  • States: loading skeleton and error with its real message.
  • Rows are 64px tall with 40px icon tiles and a chevron; no row relies on colour alone.

Chat

The classic list and its thread. Unread is a quiet count chip, never a red dot or a coloured row. Switch the list state and the thread kind; use the ⋯ menu inside a thread.

Chats list
Thread (tap ⋯ for Report and Block)
Production notes

What makes it ship-ready

  • Three thread kinds: employer (company name), your interviewer (masked, then named when the session starts), Apostrophe Support.
  • States: loading, empty (“No chats yet.” with the real explanation), error, and the reconnecting strip (“a sent message will go out when you are back”).
  • Thread: day dividers, system lines, typing dots, closing-time line, and the read-only footers for archived or expired chats.
  • Masked interviewer shows why: assigned anonymously, named when your session starts.
  • Report freezes the chat as evidence and doesn’t tell the other side; Block lists its consequences before it happens.
  • The composer’s send arrow is dimmed until there is text. There is no attach button: attachments are sent from the web for now.
