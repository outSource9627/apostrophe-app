Full interview video: new layout

Only the full interview video changes: the one an employer opens with "Watch full interview". It stays landscape, 1920×1080. The student's portrait video sits in the middle at full height, and the interviewer sits in a box in the top-right corner. The sides are a solid dark colour, the same dark as the players' background. Agora, which records the video, can't blur the sides.

1 · The frame

One fixed layout for every interview. The two pictures never overlap: there's a 64 px gap between them. Agora's docs don't say which video is drawn on top when two overlap, so this layout avoids the question.

Canvas1920 × 1080, 30 fps, landscape. The sides are #0A0A0A, the same dark as the app and website player backgrounds, so they blend into the player.
Student · centre, full heightAbout 608 × 1080, from x 656 to x 1264. Crop mode: the area is always filled, and a picture that isn't 9:16 has its centre kept.
Interviewer · top-right560 × 315 (16:9), 32 px from the top and right edges, at x 1328, y 32. Crop mode: the box is always filled.

2 · Every student camera all end up the same

The room already sends every student as 9:16 portrait. The website crops a laptop webcam's centre before sending, and the app is locked to portrait. So whatever the camera, the full video looks the same. The dashed box shows what is kept.

3 · Every interviewer camera

The interviewer sends 16:9 landscape. The website asks the browser for 1280×720, and the app is locked to landscape. The corner box is 16:9 too, so normally nothing is lost.

4 · During the interview

When someone's video stops for more than about 3.5 seconds, Agora fills their area with the background colour. Here that's the same dark as the sides, so the area simply goes dark. Sound keeps recording throughout.

5 · How it plays players unchanged

The players already show the full video as 16:9 on a dark background, so no player code changes. The dark sides of the video blend into that background. The faint dashed line marks where the video ends; it isn't on screen.
