Design option
Student app · four options per screen

Notifications · Notification settings · Your stats

Pick A, B, C or D at the top: all three phones switch to that layout approach. Use the switches above each phone for loading, empty and error. Everything is built from what the app already has; titles, bodies, times and counts are sample values for real fields.

  • Notifications (NotificationRow: kind, category, title, body, meta, read, createdAt; Mark all read; unread-only and category filter exist on the API; kept ninety days). Rows open their subject (chat, video resume, interview, applications, interests).
  • Settings (PrefRow: 8 categories, Job posts hidden; Account, Payments and Interview are locked and show “All channels”, never a disabled switch; Interests, Messages, Applications and News are per-channel switches over Push, Email and In app).
  • Your stats (Audience: shortlistCount, profileViews, openInterests, hiddenFromFeed, published). Rule 3 stays: only counts, never which employer; no chart, trend, ring, streak or badge, so the four options differ in layout and voice, not in charting.
