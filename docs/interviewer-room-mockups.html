Interviewer room — four directions

The live interview panel, redesigned four ways. Each direction is drawn for the Android app (phone) and the web (desktop browser), from the same parts the room has today: the candidate's 9:16 picture, your own 16:9 tile, the clock against the booked length and the completion mark, the assigned question script, private notes with time stamps, Flag moment, and End & score. Use the bar to switch every frame between the room's states. Tabs, Mark asked, mute, camera, Flag and End are clickable.
