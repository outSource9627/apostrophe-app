Interviewer app · Wallet · final

Wallet, ink card and request stepper

The Wallet tab of the interviewer persona, Option D from the design round, made complete. Reads the wallet, bank, ledger, withdrawals and statements endpoints (InterviewerWalletScreen and the four screens it opens). Withdraw, Payout account, Ledger and Earnings statements are the app’s own screens: tap them from the Wallet and use the back button. Amounts, names, dates, references and bank details are placeholder-shaped samples (sample “today” is Fri 2 Oct). Same type scale, tokens, hairline borders, no shadows and floating tab bar as the approved Home.
