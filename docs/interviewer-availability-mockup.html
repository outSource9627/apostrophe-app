Availability, in the approved Home and Interviews language

The Availability tab of the interviewer persona (AvailabilityScreen and OverridesScreen, reading GET /interviewers/me/availability and /availability/overview, saving with PUT /availability). One screen with three views: Weekly hours, Next 21 days, Overrides. Switch the state, then tap hours, dates, overrides and Publish.

PROPOSAL  Dashed tag: a structure the app does not have today. The app today is one day at a time: a Monday to Sunday strip, a switch, two-column hour tiles, override chips and Publish. Only the 21-date list (and the date sheet behind it) is tagged; weekly hours, the hour tiles, Publish and the overrides operations are real.
Config-driven values (slot length, the 8 AM to 10 PM page range, the booking horizon) come from /config and the availability payload; 30-minute slots and 21 days are samples. Candidate names are placeholder-shaped samples; sample “today” is Fri 2 Oct 2026.
Next 21 days = the admin setting. 21 is supply.horizonDays, returned as horizonDays; the app reads it (“next N days”) and never hardcodes it. If the server setting is another number today, an admin must set supply.horizonDays=21 for this to match. Weeks are 7 rolling days from today (2–8, 9–15, 16–22 Oct).
