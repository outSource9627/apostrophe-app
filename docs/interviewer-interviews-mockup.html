Interviews, in the approved Home language

The Interviews tab of the interviewer persona (InterviewerInterviewsScreen, reading GET /interviewers/me/interviews). Same type scale, tokens, hairline borders and floating tab bar as the approved Home. Names, times, rupee amounts and counts are placeholder-shaped samples (sample “today” is Fri 2 Oct). Switch the state, tap rows, pills and tabs. Tapping a row opens a shortened detail screen, as it does in the app today.

PROPOSAL  Dashed tag: not in the app today. Only the group headings are marked; every field shown is one the list already carries.
Config-driven values (join window, scorecard window, the “urgent” threshold, fees per tier) are admin settings in /config; the numbers here are samples.
No masking: the interviewer app shows the candidate’s full name everywhere.
